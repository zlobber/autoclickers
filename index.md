## The Hetic X.1000 program will be able to record and repeat everything you do on the computer manually.

What problems does the Hetic X.1000 solve?

Filling out forms/cards, copying documents and files, working with tables;

Searching for images by pattern and interacting with them (determining coordinates, clicking);

Working with data where repetitive manual work needs to be automated;

Working with data that requires a quick response to an event;

Automating actions in MMO games and business processes;

Automating the filling of information systems (IS);

Automating interaction with programs and their interface elements;

Automating work with Android application emulators;
